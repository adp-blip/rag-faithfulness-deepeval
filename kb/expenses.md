# Expenses

Submit expenses in Expensify within 60 days of the purchase, with a receipt for anything over 25 EUR.

Meal allowance on business travel is up to 50 EUR per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

Book flights through TravelPerk. Economy class only, except flights longer than 8 hours, where premium economy is allowed.

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transport takes more than 45 minutes longer or after 22:00.

Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll.
