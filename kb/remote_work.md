# Remote and hybrid work

Lumen Labs is hybrid. Most teams are expected in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Other days are flexible.

You can work fully remote from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays need written approval from HR because of tax and visa rules.

The company gives a one-time home office stipend of 400 EUR for new joiners, claimed through the expense tool within 90 days of your start date.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET. Meetings should not be scheduled outside these hours without the attendees agreeing.
