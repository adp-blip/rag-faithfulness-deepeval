# Equipment

Engineers get a MacBook Pro 14" or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon, their choice. Everyone else gets a MacBook Air.

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. If your laptop breaks, open a ticket in the IT portal; a loaner is available the same day in Berlin and Lisbon offices.

You may keep a monitor, keyboard and mouse at home. These come out of the 400 EUR home office stipend, not a separate budget.

When you leave the company, all equipment must be returned within 10 working days.
