# Onboarding

Your first week includes an IT setup session on day 1 and a security training you must finish within 7 days.

Every new joiner gets an onboarding buddy from another team for the first 3 months.

The probation period is 6 months. During probation the notice period is 2 weeks for both sides; after probation it becomes 1 month.

You'll have a check-in with your manager at 30, 60 and 90 days.
