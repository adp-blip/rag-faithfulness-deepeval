# Paid time off

Full-time employees get 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. Part-time employees get PTO pro-rated by contracted hours.

PTO accrues monthly, 1.83 days per month. You can carry over at most 5 unused days into the next year; anything above 5 is lost on 1 January.

Requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter requests need 2 working days notice.

Public holidays do not count against PTO. New joiners cannot take PTO during their first 30 days except for emergencies approved by their manager.
