# Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,000 EUR per calendar year for courses, books, certifications and conferences.

Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel is paid from the travel budget, not the learning budget; only the ticket counts against learning.

You also get 3 learning days per year that don't count as PTO. Book them in Workday as "Learning day".

Purchases over 300 EUR need manager approval before buying.
