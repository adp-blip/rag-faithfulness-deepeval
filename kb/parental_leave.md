# Parental leave

Birthing parents get 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents get 12 weeks of fully paid leave.

Leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption. It can be split into at most 2 blocks.

You need to be employed for at least 6 months to be eligible for the full paid leave; before that, statutory leave rules apply.

Let HR know at least 8 weeks before the planned start.
